IFS GURU — Help

How to find and fix an IFS ERP error — step by step.

1. What IFS GURU does

You hit an error in IFS ERP. Instead of digging through forums or waiting on support, you paste the error (or a screenshot) into IFS GURU. We match it against 42,000+ known IFS errors (Apps 10 and Cloud) and give you the exact steps to fix it. If an automated fix doesn't solve it, a real IFS specialist can take it on.

2. Getting started

  1. Go to the app and click Get started.
  2. Enter your email and password → you'll get a confirmation email → click the link to confirm.
  3. On "Set up your account":
    • Company / organization — enter your company name, or leave it blank if you're an independent user or contractor.
    • IFS version — pick yours (IFS10 or IFS Cloud). This makes sure we match errors for your version.
    • Click Continue.
  4. Choose a plan. Before you start, you'll be asked how you want to use IFS GURU:
    • Personal or Company subscription — unlimited automated fixes (best if you'll use it regularly)
    • Pay-as-you-go — no subscription; add a card and pay $4.99 only when you unlock a fix

(You can change this anytime — see Plans & billing below.)

Now you're in and ready to find your first fix.

3. Four ways to find a fix

Pick whichever fits how you have the error in front of you:

🔍 Troubleshoot (best if you have the error on screen)

  1. Open the Troubleshoot tab.
  2. Upload a screenshot of the IFS error (or type the error text).
  3. Click Analyze error — we read the text and find the closest matches.
  4. You'll see a match with a confidence label (Likely / Possible / Low-confidence).

🔎 Search

Type any part of the error message or code (e.g. delete not permitted) and pick the matching result.

📁 Browse by category

Explore fixes grouped by IFS module/component — useful when you're not sure of the exact error.

💬 Ask a question

Chat for general IFS guidance and pointers. (For the exact fix to a specific error, use Troubleshoot or Search.)

4. Unlocking and using a fix

When we find a match, you'll see a preview first — what the fix is, who acts, and the price — before you commit.

  • Subscribers (Personal or Company plan): click View the fix — it's free and unlimited.
  • Pay-as-you-go: click Unlock the fix — $4.99. You're charged only when you choose to unlock, and only once for that error.

Once unlocked, you'll see:

  • The step-by-step instructions
  • ⬇ Download to save them
  • 👍 / 👎 — tell us if it worked
⚠️ IFS errors touch live data. Always review a step and confirm it's right for your environment before acting, and keep your normal backups/change controls.

5. When an automated fix doesn't work — get a specialist

If you mark a fix 👎 (didn't work), you can bring in a human IFS specialist:

  1. Click Request a specialist review. (You're not charged anything yet.)
  2. A specialist reviews it and sends you a price quote (specialist help starts at $10).
  3. You'll see it in My fixes as "Awaiting your approval" — e.g. "A specialist can resolve this for $30. Approve & pay."
    • Pay-as-you-go: your earlier $4.99 is credited, so you pay only the difference.
    • You're charged only when you approve — nothing before that.
  4. The specialist delivers a fix → it shows in My fixes as "Your fix is ready — did it work?"
  5. Try it, then:
    • 👍 It worked — done.
    • 👎 Didn't work — the specialist revises and re-sends it, or it goes to review.
  6. If a specialist ultimately can't resolve it, your specialist payment is refunded in full.

6. My fixes — your history

The 💾 My fixes tab keeps everything you've unlocked:

  • Re-open any fix anytime — you're never charged again for one you've already unlocked.
  • See the status of specialist work ("Specialist review in progress", "Specialist working on it", or the delivered fix to confirm).

8. Add IFS GURU to your phone

You can put IFS GURU on your phone's home screen and open it like a regular app — full-screen, with our icon. No app store or download needed; it works on both iPhone and Android.

On iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open ifsguru.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an ↑ arrow) — it's in the toolbar next to the address bar. (If you don't see it, tap the bottom of the screen once to bring the toolbar back.)
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen → Add.

On Android (Chrome)

  1. Open ifsguru.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top-right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app) → confirm.

Now tap the IFS GURU icon anytime to open the app full-screen. Everything works the same as in your browser — find fixes, check My fixes, and manage your account on the go.

Quick FAQ

  • Do I pay before seeing anything? No — you always see a preview first and choose to unlock.
  • Will I be charged twice for the same error? No — an unlocked fix is yours to re-open free.
  • What if nothing works? A specialist can take it, and if they can't resolve it, that specialist charge is refunded.
  • Which IFS versions? IFS Apps 10 and IFS Cloud — set yours in your account so matches fit your version.

How to help users, deliver fixes, and get paid — step by step.

Who this is for

You're an experienced IFS person. On IFS GURU, when a user's automated fix doesn't solve their error, you can take it on: quote a price, deliver a fix, and earn a share of what the user pays. You work when and how you like — you're an independent specialist, not an employee.

How a job reaches you (the user's side, in brief)

You don't need to read the user guide — here's all you need to know about where jobs come from:

Users come to IFS GURU with an IFS ERP error. They first try our automated fixes (a matched library solution or an AI-assembled one — via subscription or a small per-fix charge). If an automated fix doesn't solve it, they escalate — and that's where you come in:

  • Their escalation becomes a job in one of your modules.
  • They see your quote and must approve and pay before you start — so by the time a job is In progress, the user has already committed.
  • After you deliver, the user tries your fix and either confirms it worked, or says it didn't (which sends it back to you to revise, or to a dispute).
  • If it ultimately can't be resolved, the user is refunded — which is why your credit stays pending until they confirm.

That's the whole user side. The rest of this guide is your workflow.

1. Create your account, then become a consultant

A consultant account is a normal account with consultant powers added — so you sign up like any user first.

Create your account:

  1. Go to the app → Get started → enter your email + password → click the confirmation link in your email → log in.
  2. On "Set up your account," fill in your details (company optional; pick your IFS version) → Continue.
  3. You'll land on a "choose a plan" screen — you don't need to buy a plan to work as a consultant. Just go straight to the 💼 Consultant tab.

(If you also want to use IFS GURU yourself — for example, to get help with an IFS module outside your own expertise — you can pick a plan anytime. Pay-as-you-go is fine: you'd just add a card and pay per fix. It's not needed to take consultant jobs.)

Apply as a consultant:

  1. In the 💼 Consultant tab, click Become a consultant.
  2. Pick your modules — the IFS areas you know (e.g. Purchasing, Shop Order, Finance). You'll only be offered jobs in these areas. (Hold Ctrl/Cmd to select several.)
  3. Click Apply.
  4. An admin reviews new consultants once. When you're approved, you can start taking jobs.

(You can add or remove your modules anytime from the Consultant tab — no re-approval needed.)

2. How getting paid works (read this first)

The flow is designed so users trust it and you're paid fairly:

  1. A user escalates an error → you send a price quote.
  2. The user approves and pays → the job is yours.
  3. You deliver a fix directly to the user.
  4. The user confirms it worked (or it auto-confirms after 7 days of no response) → your credit becomes available to cash out.
  5. You request a cash-out once you reach the minimum, and an admin pays you off-platform.

Your share is your tier percentage of what the user paid:

TierYour share
🥉 Bronze70%
🥈 Silver75%
🥇 Gold80%
Important: credit for a delivered-but-unconfirmed fix is pending — it's not cashable until the user confirms (or the 7-day window passes). This protects both sides.

3. Find and quote a job

  1. In the 💼 Consultant tab, look under Open jobs — these are escalations in your modules. You're also notified when a matching job comes in.
  2. Click Send a quote on a job.
  3. Enter your price — anywhere from $10 to $200 — and submit.
  4. The user reviews it and either approves and pays (the job moves to In progress and appears under My jobs) or declines (it returns to the queue).

(Higher tiers get first crack at new jobs — see Tiers below.)

4. Deliver the fix

  1. Under My jobs, find your In progress job and click Deliver fix.
  2. Fill in:
    • Title — a short name for the fix
    • Who acts — who performs the steps (e.g. Buyer, Planner)
    • Steps — the fix, one step per line
  3. Click Deliver to user.

The fix goes straight to the user to try. Your credit is now pending until they confirm.

(You can re-open View my fix anytime to see exactly what you sent.)

5. If the user says it didn't work

When a user marks your fix 👎, the job comes back to you with their feedback. You have two choices:

  • Revise & re-deliver — click it, tweak your steps (the form is pre-filled with what you sent), and deliver again for the user to re-check.
  • Dispute — if you're confident the fix is correct, click "I'm confident it works — dispute." An admin reviews it and the user's feedback and makes the call. If they side with you, your credit becomes available; if not, the user is refunded.

6. Get paid

First, set your payout details. In the 💼 Consultant tab there's a 💳 Payout details box — enter your PayPal or Interac e-transfer email (or how you'd like to be paid) and click Save. You need this on file before you can request a cash-out (it's how we know where to send your money).

In the same tab, your credit box shows two numbers:

  • Available to cash out — confirmed jobs (cashable now)
  • Pending user confirmation — delivered jobs still within the confirmation window

When your available balance reaches the minimum (shown in the app), click 💸 Request cash-out. That snapshots your available balance into a request; an admin pays it out off-platform (e.g. e-transfer/PayPal) and marks it paid. You'll get a notification when it's paid.

7. Tiers & priority

Your tier (Bronze → Silver → Gold) does two things:

  • Higher share of each job (70% → 75% → 80%)
  • First access to new jobs — a new job opens to Gold immediately, then Silver, then Bronze a bit later. So quality work that earns you a higher tier also gets you first pick.

How you move up: everyone starts at Bronze, and the IFS GURU team promotes you to Silver, then Gold, as you build a track record. Here's exactly what earns a bump:

  • Fixes that work — the biggest factor is your confirmation rate: how often users mark your delivered fix 👍 "it worked." A consistently high rate is what moves you up.
  • Few rejections or disputes — the occasional 👎 is normal, but a clean record (rare rejections, no lost disputes) signals reliability.
  • A solid volume of confirmed fixes — enough completed jobs to show the quality is consistent, not a one-off.
  • Responsiveness — revising promptly when a user asks, and communicating clearly.

In short: deliver fixes that reliably work, and the tier follows. It's about quality and consistency, not just how many jobs you take — and because tiers are reviewed by a person (not an automatic score), there's no number to game. Just do good work, and you'll move up.

8. The rules

  • You can't take your own job. If you escalated an error as a user, you won't see or be able to quote it as a consultant.
  • User data is confidential. Use the error details only to resolve that user's issue on the platform. Don't copy, keep, or share it, and don't contact users off-platform.
  • Do good work. Deliver competent, professional fixes and respond to reasonable revision requests — the user's confirmation is what releases your pay, and your tier is built on a good track record.
  • You're an independent contractor. You accept the consultant agreement before taking jobs, and you're responsible for your own taxes on what you earn. (See the consultant agreement for details.)

Quick FAQ

  • When do I actually get paid? After the user confirms your fix (or 7 days pass), your credit becomes available; then you request a cash-out and an admin pays you.
  • What if the user never responds? After 7 days it auto-confirms and your credit becomes available.
  • What if the user is wrong and my fix was right? Revise, or dispute it for admin review.
  • Can I choose which errors I work on? Yes — you only see jobs in the modules you selected, and you choose which to quote.
  • How much do I earn? Your tier share (70–80%) of what the user pays for the job.
